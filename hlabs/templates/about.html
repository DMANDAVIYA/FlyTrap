About HackUMass Labs

HackUMass Labs started in 2024 in Amherst, Massachusetts, with one idea: teams building AI products should spend their time on models and data, not on GPU clusters.

Today we host more than a thousand private models for robotics, healthcare, logistics and analytics companies, and serve billions of tokens a month from our own data centers.

Security

Customer weights and data are isolated per namespace and encrypted at rest. API tokens are scoped and revocable. Report a vulnerability to security@hackumass-labs.example.
