FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of the Growth plan with 5M tokens, no card required. At the end of the trial you pick a plan or your deployments pause. Nothing is deleted.

Do you train on my data or my prompts?

No. Your weights, datasets and requests are yours. We never use them to train anything, and request bodies are only kept for the log retention window of your plan.

What happens if I go over my included tokens?

Extra usage is billed at the per-million rates above. You can set a monthly budget per key and we will alert you, or pause traffic, when you reach it.

Can I bring my own model?

Yes. Upload safetensors or GGUF weights with the CLI, or connect a private repository. Most architectures under 70B parameters are supported out of the box.

How do invoices and payment work?

Invoices are issued on the first of each month and are payable by card or ACH within 15 days. Scale customers can pay annually by invoice.
