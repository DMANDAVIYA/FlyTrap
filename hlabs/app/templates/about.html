About us

We make running your own model boring.

HackUMass Labs started in 2024 in a shared office above a bookshop in Amherst, Massachusetts. We were three engineers who had each spent too many nights babysitting GPU servers, and we wanted a platform where deploying a fine-tuned model felt as routine as deploying a web app.

What we believe

Your model, your data, your call.

Open and private models are now good enough for most products, but running them well still takes a dedicated infrastructure team. We want any team of two to get the same reliability as a team of two hundred.

We keep our promises small and specific: predictable prices, endpoints that stay up, and a clear line that your weights, datasets and requests are never used to train anything. When something breaks, we tell you what happened and what we changed.
